Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

TRACKR is an activity tracker available on the web, iOS and Android. This policy explains what information TRACKR collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and the name you enter when you create an account.
  • Your content: the lists, goals and activity entries you create, along with the time each entry was logged.
  • Settings on your device: your profile name, tagline and theme preference are stored on your device only.

TRACKR does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers.

How we use it

Your information is used only to provide the app: signing you in and storing and syncing your lists and entries across your devices. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Account and list data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Access is restricted so that only you can read or change your own lists and entries.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the TRACKR app (Profile → Delete Account). This permanently deletes your account, lists and entries. You can also delete individual lists and entries at any time.

Children

TRACKR is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
