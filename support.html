Common questions

How do goals work?

When you create a list, choose how it counts:

  • Count Up adds one for each entry. Set an optional goal (like 30) and you'll get a celebration when you reach it.
  • Count Down starts from a number you choose (like 10) and each entry takes one away. You'll be celebrated when it hits zero, and you can keep logging past it.

Can I change a list after creating it?

Yes. Open the list and tap Edit to change its name, activity type, icon, counting mode or goal.

How do I hide a list without deleting it?

Open the list and tap Archive. Archived lists move to the Archived section at the bottom of your Lists screen, where you can tap Restore to bring them back. Your entries are kept.

How do I delete an entry or a list?

Tap the × next to an entry to remove it. To delete a whole list and all its entries, open the list and tap Delete. Deleting can't be undone.

Are my lists on all my devices?

Yes. Your lists are saved to your account, so they appear wherever you log in — the iPhone and Android apps, and on the web at trackr.agilelifemgmt.com.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll help you get back in.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, lists and entries. If you can't access the app, email us from your account's address and we'll delete it for you.
